How to read this. Placement follows the model's own level semantics, not vocabulary matching: a thesis sits where its function belongs. One location may hold many theses. A location is empty only when no thesis from the corpus performs that function at all — that is the claim being made, and it is falsifiable by producing a thesis that belongs there. Proposals are marked by who would author them: AI where the gap is structural and derivable from the form, human where it needs mandate or domain authority, co-produced where AI structures the gap and humans supply evidence.
